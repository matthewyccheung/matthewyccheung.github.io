I'm a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rice University, working on machine learning, LLM reasoning, and computer vision.

My research focuses on developing and evaluating AI models, with particular interests in foundation-model reasoning, uncertainty quantification, generative modeling, and reliable decision-making. I've published first-author research at ICLR, EMNLP (Outstanding Paper), and MICCAI, and have industry research experience developing medical AI at Johnson & Johnson and software development experience developing manufacturing software at Enovix Corporation (pre-IPO).

### What I Can Build and Deliver

I develop practical ML solutions that help improve product performance, automate complex workflows, reduce costly failures, and accelerate R&D. Examples from my research and industry experience include:

1. **Improve LLM reasoning and reduce failures:** Developed [CROP (EMNLP 2026, Outstanding Paper 🏆)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1776/), a method for identifying trustworthy reasoning steps and detecting where model reasoning becomes unreliable-relevant to improving AI assistants and agents.

2. **Automate image analysis:** Worked at Johnson & Johnson R&D to develop and deploy automated detection of geographic atrophy biomarkers from 3D optical coherence tomography scans [GAIZ (MLMI MICCAI Oral 🗣️)](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/MLMI_029.html) and fundus autofluorescence images, with potential to reduce manual analysis and support scalable imaging workflows.

3. **Make computer vision models more dependable:** Developed [COMPASS (ICLR 2026)](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10006913) to quantify segmentation quality and reliability under distribution shifts, helping identify predictions that may require additional review. Developed [ConVOLT (MICCAI 2026, Spotlight 💡)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-38085-2_17), [ConRad](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08084) and [Bias-CP (ISBI Oral 🗣️)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11515847), enabling computationally efficient uncertainty quantification for volumetric, radiomic and radiotherapy planning measurements in medical-image segmentation.

4. **Evaluate generative models and uncover hidden failures:** Studied diffusion-based CT reconstruction under sparse measurements, identifying when generated images recover useful information and when plausible-looking outputs may be misleading -- [Diffusion Priors (ISBI)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10981058).

5. **Develop reproducible ML research tools:** Released Python implementations and experimental pipelines for published ML methods, supporting testing, benchmarking, and further model development.
