Selected publications and research highlights.

Research Highlights

  1. Under Review
    Efficient Conformal Volumetry for Template-Based Segmentation
    Under Review, 2026
    arXiv GitHub
    Efficient conformal uncertainty quantification for volumetry in template-based segmentation pipelines, producing prediction sets with guaranteed coverage while staying practical for large-scale medical imaging workflows.
  2. ICLR
    COMPASS: Robust Feature Conformal Prediction for Medical Segmentation Metrics
    ICLR, 2026
    OpenReview arXiv GitHub
    A feature-based conformal framework for building reliable prediction intervals and sets for segmentation metrics, designed to remain robust under distribution shift and aligned with clinical evaluation metrics.
  3. IEEE ISBI
    Bias-Aware Conformal Prediction for Metric-Based Imaging Pipelines
    IEEE ISBI, 2026
    arXiv
    Conformal calibration for metric-based imaging pipelines with explicit attention to bias, aiming to preserve statistical guarantees while accounting for heterogeneous errors across cohorts and measurement conditions.
  4. UNSURE @ MICCAI
    Metric-Guided Conformal Bounds for Probabilistic Image Reconstruction
    UNSURE @ MICCAI, 2025
    Springer arXiv GitHub
    Instead of calibrating pixel-level uncertainty, this work calibrates conformal bounds on clinically meaningful derived metrics from probabilistic reconstructions, enabling interpretable uncertainty and flagging plausible-looking but statistically unlikely reconstructions.
  5. IEEE ISBI
    When Are Diffusion Priors Helpful in Sparse Reconstruction? A Study With Sparse-View CT
    IEEE ISBI, 2025
    IEEE arXiv
    A systematic study of diffusion priors for sparse-view CT showing that diffusion can recover detail with very few projections, but can also plateau and produce convincing failure modes that require careful evaluation in high-stakes settings.

Other Publications

  1. IEEE TBME
    Wearable Blood Pressure Monitoring Devices: Understanding Heterogeneity in Design and Evaluation
    IEEE TBME, 2024
    IEEE Website
    A systematic review of cuffless wearable blood pressure devices, emphasizing how protocol and evaluation design choices drive heterogeneous reported performance and proposing directions for more comparable and safer validation.
  2. IEEE TBCAS
    An Ultrasonic Transceiver for Non-Invasive Intracranial Pressure Sensing
    IEEE TBCAS, 2024
    IEEE
    A low-power ultrasonic through-transmission transceiver for portable, non-invasive intracranial pressure monitoring using time-of-flight measurements, validated on phantom setups with strong correlation to invasive sensing.
  3. IEEE ISSCC
    17.11 A 9mW Ultrasonic Through Transmission Transceiver for Non-Invasive Intracranial Pressure Sensing
    IEEE ISSCC, 2024
    IEEE
    An energy-efficient ultrasonic through-transmission transceiver architecture for non-invasive intracranial pressure sensing, focused on enabling practical, portable monitoring hardware.
  4. IEEE ICASSP
    Wearing a MASK: Compressed Representations of Variable-Length Sequences Using Recurrent Neural Tangent Kernels
    IEEE ICASSP, 2021
    IEEE arXiv
    A representation method for variable-length sequences based on recurrent neural tangent kernels, producing fixed-length compressed features for downstream learning tasks.
